# Preventative and Therapeutic Potential of Coumarin Compounds From Hemidesmus indicus Against Peptic Ulcer Disease

## About the Project

This academic research project investigates the preventive and therapeutic potential of coumarin compounds from *Hemidesmus indicus* against peptic ulcer disease.

## Objectives

- To study the medicinal properties of *Hemidesmus indicus*.
- To prepare and extract bioactive compounds from the plant root.
- To identify and confirm the presence of coumarin compounds.
- To study the potential anti-ulcer properties of the extracted compounds.

## Methodology

1. Collection and preparation of *Hemidesmus indicus* root
2. Drying and grinding of the plant material
3. Soxhlet extraction
4. Phytochemical screening
5. Coumarin confirmation test
6. TLC analysis
7. Evaluation of potential anti-ulcer activity

## Techniques Used

- Soxhlet Extraction
- Phytochemical Screening
- Qualitative Coumarin Test
- Thin Layer Chromatography (TLC)
- UV Visualization
- Rf Value Calculation
